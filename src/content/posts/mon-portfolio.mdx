Building a **modern professional portfolio** has become essential to present your projects, your background and your technical watch.
In this article I share the steps of building my personal site — from choosing the domain name to automated deployment.

---

## Step 1 — Buying the domain name

The first step was to **reserve my domain name**.
I chose [OVH](https://www.ovh.com) for its simplicity and affordable prices, and registered the domain **`aemanemouteirou.com`**.

That gave me a clean, professional address for my portfolio, while leaving room to connect future projects.

---

## Step 2 — Starting from a template

Rather than starting from scratch, I began with an open-source template I found elegant and well structured: **[Magic Portfolio](https://github.com/once-ui-system/magic-portfolio)**, based on **Next.js** and **Once UI**.

It gave me a solid base, with a clear architecture, content management through **MDX** (ideal for my articles) and a clean design.

---

## Step 3 — Customising design and content

I then **customised the template** to fit my own world:

- Adjusting colours, fonts and styles to reflect my visual identity
- Adding my projects (my C and JavaFX work, my AI projects)
- Writing MDX articles to detail what I built
- Configuring SEO metadata (titles, descriptions, social tags)

---

## Step 4 — Deploying with Cloudflare Pages

To host the site I chose **[Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/)** — a free, fast solution.
The **GitHub** integration is direct: connect the repository and Cloudflare handles the rest.

As soon as I **push code to the `main` branch**, the site is rebuilt and the new version goes live within seconds.
That gives **continuous updates** without any manual step.

---

## Step 5 — Security and maintenance

Before going live I made sure to:

- build in **production** mode
- add the necessary **environment variables** in Cloudflare
- check the **security headers** (CSP, HTTPS, etc.)

The result: a fast, secure, fully automated site.

---

## Update — version 2 with Astro

In November 2026 I rebuilt the site from scratch with **Astro**. The content (MDX) moved over almost unchanged, but the site now ships **no framework JavaScript**, generates its pages at build time and sets its security headers through a simple `_headers` file. The homepage animation is a small piece of plain canvas code that turns a cloud of dots into the story of one of my projects.

The lesson: choose the tool that fits the content. A portfolio is mostly text and images, so a static-first framework is a better match than a full application framework.

---

## Wrap-up

Building a portfolio isn't just publishing a few projects: it is a **real personal product**, designed, structured and automated.

Thanks to **Cloudflare Pages** and a continuous-deployment setup, I have a **free, fast and scalable** delivery chain.
