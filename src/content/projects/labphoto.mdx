## Context

The Lab Photo Dauphine is the university's film-photography association: an analogue and digital darkroom, workshops, outings and an annual photo competition. I am still a member and I still look after its website.

The site had grown on WordPress. I am rebuilding it around one constraint: **the people who maintain it are volunteers, not developers, and they change every year.**

<Callout tone="brand">The design question is not "what is the best stack?" but "what will still be easy for someone who joins the committee next September?"</Callout>

## Migrating the content

The existing WordPress content was imported once through a re-runnable script that does not create duplicates: **38 documents and 87 images**. The old WordPress backup is kept, unpublished, until the new site is validated.

## Making it usable by non-developers

I wrote an editors' guide in plain language, with ready-made recipes: add a new competition (the previous edition moves to the archives by itself), change the committee in September, add photos by drag and drop, show an announcement on the homepage, set a price. The guide also reminds contributors to publish only photos they have the authors' agreement for.

## Status

The new site is built and the go-live checklist (CMS project, deploy hook, contact form, domain switch) is documented. It is in progress rather than finished, and I will update this page when the switch is done.
