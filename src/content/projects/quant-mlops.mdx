<Text>
The <strong>Quant-MLOps</strong> project aims to make quantitative-finance model development
<strong>automated, traceable and reproducible</strong>. It draws on MLOps good practice to orchestrate
the whole model lifecycle — from raw data to a deployed, monitored model.
</Text>

## Context & motivation

<Table
  data={{
    headers: [
      { content: "Problem", key: "pb" },
      { content: "Observation", key: "ctx" }

    rows: [
      ["Lack of integration", "Quantitative-finance research environments remain fragmented (isolated scripts, untraceable runs)."],
      ["Limited reproducibility", "Backtests are not systematically versioned or comparable."],

To address this, <strong>Quant-MLOps</strong> proposes a complete ecosystem to <em>collect, transform, train, evaluate, deploy and monitor</em> quantitative models at scale.
</Text>

## Operational flow

  data={{

## Challenges & lessons

<Text>
The project calls for careful coordination between services (<strong>Airflow, MLflow, Feast</strong>),
Docker dependency management, and keeping offline and online data consistent.
Building reliable monitoring (Prometheus + Grafana) and a secure CI/CD is strengthening my grasp of
<strong>MLOps patterns</strong> and distributed architectures.
</Text>
